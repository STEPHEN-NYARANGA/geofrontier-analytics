About GeoFrontier Analytics

GeoFrontier Analytics is a proposed geospatial science and technology business with an initial focus on Africa and broader Global South applications.

Why GeoFrontier?

Earth observation and geospatial technologies can reveal patterns that are difficult to understand from the ground alone. But useful insight requires more than imagery or a sophisticated model: it requires good questions, appropriate methods, clear uncertainty and a strong understanding of local context.

GeoFrontier Analytics is being developed to bring these elements together in practical consulting, research partnerships and decision-support tools.

Our principles

Scientific care. We favour transparent methodologies, quality checks and honest communication about limitations.

Local relevance. Methods and deliverables should fit the institutions, environments and decisions they are meant to serve.

Useful delivery. Maps, dashboards, models and reports should help people act—not merely look impressive.
