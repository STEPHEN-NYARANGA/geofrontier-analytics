About GeoFrontier

Scientific care, with a practical outlook.

GeoFrontier Analytics is taking shape as a specialist consultancy in Earth observation, geospatial science and data analytics, with an initial focus on Kenya and wider African applications.

Good analysis should be understandable, reproducible and honest about what the evidence can show.

The founder

Experience across geophysics, climate data and applied computing.

GeoFrontier is being developed by Stephen Nyaranga. His public portfolio includes satellite radar research, climate analysis, scientific computing and collaborative digital projects.

The consultancy builds on this technical foundation while keeping personal academic work separate from commercial delivery. We aim to work with organisations that value clarity, robust methods and a strong understanding of local context.
