About GeoFrontier

Scientific care, with a practical outlook.

GeoFrontier Analytics is taking shape as a specialist consultancy in Earth observation, geospatial science and data analytics, with an initial focus on Kenya and wider African applications.

Good analysis should be understandable, reproducible and honest about what the evidence can show.

Founder

Stephen Nyaranga

Founder, GeoFrontier Analytics

GeoFrontier Analytics was established as a concept by Stephen Nyaranga to bring scientific methods and geospatial technologies closer to real decisions. The consultancy is being developed around Earth observation, geospatial analysis, environmental intelligence and responsible applications of AI.

Its approach is straightforward: understand the question, examine the available evidence, choose appropriate methods and communicate findings clearly. Professional backgrounds and future company engagements will be presented separately.
