About GeoFrontier

Geospatial science with purpose and accountability.

GeoFrontier Analytics is being developed as a scientific consultancy focused on Earth observation, climate and environmental monitoring, forecasting support, spatial analytics and applied AI, with a planned base in Kenya and a wider Global South outlook.

Good analysis should be understandable, reproducible and honest about what the evidence can show.

Who we aim to be

Our mission and vision

Our mission

Make geospatial science useful where decisions are made.

Our mission is to translate Earth observation, environmental monitoring, climate forecast interpretation, GIS and responsible data analysis into clear evidence for organisations addressing environmental, infrastructure and development challenges.

Our vision

Scientific insight that serves the Global South.

Our vision is to become a trusted, Africa-rooted geospatial consultancy known for transparent methods, practical solutions and partnerships that strengthen local expertise.

Strategic direction

What we are working towards

These are proposed goals, not achievements we claim to have completed.

Credible scientific delivery

Build monitoring, forecasting and geospatial analysis projects around clear questions, reliable data, appropriate validation and documented uncertainty.

Useful decision support

Develop maps, environmental monitoring workflows, interpreted climate outlooks and accessible tools that respond to practical institutional needs.

Responsible business growth

Establish the legal, financial and operational foundations required for dependable consulting work.

Collaborative partnerships

Pursue partnerships with public institutions, universities, development organisations and specialist consultancies.

Capacity development

Support knowledge transfer through documentation, training and maintainable geospatial tools.

Local relevance, wider reach

Begin with opportunities in Kenya and East Africa while keeping our methods adaptable to other Global South contexts.

Company values

What will guide our work

Scientific integrity

Use appropriate methods, cite sources and describe uncertainty without exaggeration.

Context before technology

Understand local conditions and the decisions behind a project before selecting tools.

Responsible data practice

Protect confidential information, respect licences and consider possible misuse of location data.

Clarity and accessibility

Make technical findings understandable to people who need to use them.

Collaboration

Value partnerships, clear responsibilities and the expertise of local stakeholders.

Practical usefulness

Prioritise reproducible maps, reports and tools that support meaningful next steps.

Founder

Stephen Nyaranga

Founder, GeoFrontier Analytics

GeoFrontier Analytics was conceived by Stephen Nyaranga to bring scientific methods and geospatial technologies closer to real decisions. The consultancy is being developed around Earth observation, flood and environmental monitoring, climate forecasting support, geospatial analysis and responsible applications of AI.

Its approach is straightforward: understand the question, examine the available evidence, choose appropriate methods and communicate findings clearly. Company projects and appointments will be published only when appropriately documented and approved.

Business readiness

Building the foundations with care.

GeoFrontier Analytics is a proposed consultancy. We will verify incorporation, supplier eligibility and applicable regulatory obligations before publishing formal registration details or describing the business as eligible for a particular procurement.

Intended commitments

  • Confirm company registration and business particulars before making official claims.
  • Assess relevant tax, procurement and data protection requirements before work begins.
  • Use written scopes, transparent deliverables and agreed intellectual property arrangements.
  • Obtain consent before sharing client work, partner names or sensitive spatial information.

Official references: Kenya Business Registration Service, e-GP Kenya and Data Protection Commissioner.
