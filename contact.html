Preparing an enquiry

A useful first brief can be short.

The geographic area

Describe the region, site or administrative area, and any boundaries or coordinates already available.

The decision or challenge

Explain what needs to be understood, monitored, mapped or communicated, and for whom.

Available information

Note existing imagery, GIS data, reports, field records and any confidentiality or licence restrictions.

Intended output and timing

Tell us whether you need maps, data layers, a report, training or a prototype, and any important deadline.

Potential partnerships

Good geospatial work is often collaborative.

We welcome discussions with organisations whose expertise and local knowledge complement scientific and technical analysis.

Public and development programmes

Early discussions about data needs, responsible spatial evidence and possible future consulting arrangements, subject to appropriate registration and eligibility.

Research and education

Technical collaboration, training, methods exchange or support for applied geospatial analysis, with clear attribution and data permissions.

Engineering and specialist consultancies

Exploratory subcontracting and multidisciplinary project discussions where geospatial expertise adds practical value.
