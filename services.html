Our expertise

Geospatial science that meets practical needs.

We work across satellite remote sensing, GIS, climate analysis and scientific computing. Each assignment begins with the data available and the decisions the work must support.

Our services are developed around evidence, appropriate methods and clear deliverables. We do not promise a model can answer what the data cannot support.
Geography and environment

Earth observation and GIS

Satellite data can help show where landscapes are changing, how land is being used and where new patterns deserve closer attention. We combine image analysis with geospatial data management and mapping.

  • Land-use and land-cover analysis
  • Satellite image processing and change detection
  • GIS datasets, mapping and spatial analysis
  • Technical documentation and reproducible workflows
Infrastructure and energy

Ground motion and radar interferometry

Interferometric synthetic aperture radar can reveal subtle land-surface movement across time. We evaluate suitable satellite radar methods for exploratory ground-motion analysis, with attention to data quality and limitations.

  • Multi-temporal InSAR analysis
  • Ground deformation mapping and interpretation
  • Remote-sensing quality checks and uncertainty reporting
  • Support for research and exploratory site assessments
Climate and sustainability

Climate and environmental analytics

From historical climate observations to environmental indicators, we help organise and analyse information that can support resilience planning and applied research.

  • Climate-data processing and visualisation
  • Environmental and spatial risk screening
  • Reproducible analyses and technical reports
  • Research support and stakeholder communication
Digital tools and data science

Geospatial data engineering and AI

Some questions need a clear map. Others benefit from a reliable analytical workflow or a machine-learning model. We choose tools according to their value and validate results before drawing conclusions.

  • Python-based geospatial pipelines
  • Applied modelling and machine learning
  • Interactive maps and lightweight decision tools
  • Data quality, visualisation and technical training
