Our capabilities

Services designed around the problem.

We combine Earth observation, spatial science and AI in focused projects. Each engagement starts with the decisions you need to make, not a predefined technology.

01 · Climate and risk

Flood and environmental intelligence

Earth observation, terrain analytics and geospatial workflows that help investigate flooding, changing environmental conditions and exposure. Work may include satellite-derived flood extents, hazard screening and evidence-based reporting, with validation appropriate to the application.

02 · Monitoring

Mining, energy and infrastructure

InSAR-based surface deformation analysis, geological context, change detection and geospatial assessment for infrastructure and resource-sector questions. Operational suitability and accuracy depend on data quality and study design.

03 · Earth data

Remote sensing and GIS

Land-use and land-cover analysis, spatial data harmonisation, environmental monitoring, geospatial database preparation, mapping and custom spatial analysis for research and planning.

04 · Software and AI

Geospatial AI and WebGIS

Task-specific machine learning, repeatable analytical pipelines, interactive web mapping and dashboards. We prioritise model evaluation, data provenance and usable outputs over AI claims.

Delivery approach

From question to usable evidence.

Define the decision and scope → assess data and constraints → develop the analysis → validate and document results → communicate findings and hand over usable outputs.
