Image and video provenance

Science deserves a visible source.

These photos, maps and animations are documentary examples from scientific agencies. They are included to explain geospatial applications, not to imply GeoFrontier has conducted the depicted missions, surveys or client projects.

NASA imagery must be used in line with NASA's media guidance. NASA and USGS logos are not part of the GeoFrontier brand, and no agency endorsement is implied.

Any future client or education photography will only be added with suitable permissions, safeguarding reviews and truthful descriptions. No person shown in a USGS photo is described as a GeoFrontier employee or participant.
