Geophysics and energyOlkaria, Kenya

Geophysics and energy 2023 to 2024

Investigating land movement at the Olkaria geothermal field

Completed as part of MSc geophysics research, this study used Sentinel-1 InSAR time-series analysis to examine ground deformation in Kenya's Olkaria geothermal field over the 2020 to 2024 period.

The work demonstrates experience with satellite radar processing, spatial interpretation and technical research in an energy setting. Specific deformation rates are not reproduced here because the underlying thesis figures have not been independently reviewed for this website.

Context: Academic research associated with ICTP-EAIFR. This is not a GeoFrontier client project.

Climate innovationAccra, Ghana

Climate innovation 2026

HeatAlert Accra and practical heat resilience

Working with Team PUM-CLID, the founder contributed to a project combining an LSTM-based wet-bulb globe temperature forecast with concepts for cooling shelters and wearable protection.

The team received Best Innovative Accelerator recognition during the Imperial and AIMS Global Fellows programme. The cooling concepts are presented here as innovations, not independently validated commercial products.

Context: Collaborative team work. The award and project are attributed to the team.

Seismology and digital tools 2026

A seismic information and alerting demonstration

Developed during the Geodesy for a Resilient Africa Summer School, this application demonstrates seismic data visualisation and an interface for displaying alerts.

The project is a learning and prototyping exercise, not a certified early-warning service or an operational public safety system.

Climate researchWest Antarctica

Climate research 2021 to 2022

Atmospheric rivers and surface melting on Pine Island Glacier

This MSc research examined the influence of atmospheric rivers on surface melting at Pine Island Glacier during the 1983 and 1998 periods.

It reflects experience in working with climate datasets and interpreting physical processes. Quantitative results are left to the thesis rather than summarised without the underlying figures.

Context: Academic research at AIMS Senegal.
