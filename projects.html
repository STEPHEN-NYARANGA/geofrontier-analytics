Research and demonstrations

Research and applications

Our portfolio is being developed. We will publish approved case studies with clear data provenance, methods and limitations rather than claim commercial projects that have not taken place.

Planned case study

Radar-based flood mapping

Potential case study exploring Sentinel-1 radar observations, terrain constraints, uncertainty and reference validation in flood-prone landscapes. Public examples will use only data cleared for sharing.

Planned outputs: map series, methodology summary, validation and limitations.

Planned case study

Surface deformation monitoring

Potential demonstration of multi-temporal InSAR methods for surface movement near infrastructure or resource extraction sites.

Planned outputs: deformation visualisations, quality-control summary and interpretation.

Planned case study

Landscape and land-use change

Potential analysis using openly licensed satellite observations to communicate changes in land cover and the implications for environmental management.

Planned outputs: change maps, temporal summaries and interactive layers.

What to expect

Transparent technical evidence

Each future study will identify data sources, processing methods, validation, caveats and the difference between research results and operational decision-support products.
