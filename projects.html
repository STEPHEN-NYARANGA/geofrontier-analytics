01 / GEOTHERMAL ENERGY · SATELLITE RADAR

Ground deformation at the Olkaria geothermal field

Kenya · MSc Geophysics research · 2023–2024

Sentinel-1 radar time-series analysis was used to investigate ground deformation in and around the Olkaria geothermal field over the 2020–2024 period. The work demonstrates the use of satellite interferometry for studying change in energy-producing landscapes.

Approach: InSAR time-series processing, deformation interpretation and geospatial analysis. Project-specific findings and uncertainty figures are not reproduced here without the original thesis figures.

Origin: Academic research completed at ICTP-EAIFR, not a GeoFrontier consultancy contract.

02 / HEAT RISK · CLIMATE INNOVATION

HeatAlert Accra — SolarCool CoolTree

Ghana · Collaborative innovation project · 2026

Developed with Team PUM-CLID, this project connected an LSTM-based wet-bulb globe temperature forecast with ideas for protective cooling infrastructure and wearable technology. It received a Best Innovative Accelerator recognition at the Imperial–AIMS Global Fellows programme.

Components: Heat forecast, CoolShade shelter concept and CoolBand wearable concept. Product readiness and field effectiveness are not independently established here.

Origin: Team project; recognition belongs to the team, not GeoFrontier Analytics.

03 / SEISMOLOGY · DATA VISUALISATION

Seismic early-warning demonstration

Cameroon · Geodesy for a Resilient Africa Summer School · June–July 2026

A demonstration application developed during a geodesy summer school, exploring seismic data visualisation and alerting interfaces. It highlights how scientific signals may be communicated in accessible web tools.

Scope: Training and demonstration project. It is not described as a certified or operational public-warning system.

Origin: Founder’s educational project, not a GeoFrontier client deployment.

04 / CLIMATE RESEARCH · POLAR ENVIRONMENTS

Atmospheric rivers and melting on Pine Island Glacier

West Antarctica · MSc research · 2021–2022

Research investigating the influence of atmospheric rivers on surface melting at Pine Island Glacier during the 1983 and 1998 periods. The study demonstrates experience with climate datasets and physical interpretation beyond regional geospatial applications.

Focus: Climate processes and extreme moisture transport. No quantitative findings are claimed here without reference to the thesis.

Origin: MSc research at AIMS Senegal, outside GeoFrontier Analytics.
