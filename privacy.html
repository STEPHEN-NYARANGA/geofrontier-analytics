Website information

Privacy and information notice

How this early GeoFrontier website currently works and what visitors should know before sharing information.

This notice describes the website as presently configured; it is not a claim of company registration, regulatory certification or a complete policy for future client work.

Who operates this site

GeoFrontier Analytics is a proposed scientific consultancy being developed by its founder, Stephen Nyaranga. Dedicated company legal and contact particulars will be published after they have been established and verified.

Information collected through this website

The website currently has no active enquiry form, account registration, payment system or site-operated analytics tracking script. You can browse its pages without entering your personal details. The site is published through GitHub Pages, which processes technical requests as part of serving website content.

External services and links

The homepage uses an Earth photograph served from Wikimedia Commons; the About page loads the founder's portrait from his website; and the forecasting initiative shows an image hosted on GitHub's raw content service. Other NASA and USGS examples are predominantly hosted as optimised files within this GitHub Pages site. External hosts may receive ordinary browser requests when third-party images are loaded. External links to LinkedIn, GitHub and scientific data portals are governed by those services' own privacy practices.

Enquiries and sensitive information

Our Contact page currently links to the founder's professional LinkedIn account. If you communicate through that service, its terms and privacy practices apply. Please do not send confidential client datasets, location-sensitive files, personal records or credentials through an unsolicited message.

Future updates

Before introducing a dedicated business contact form, cookies, analytics or client data services, this website notice and the relevant data-handling processes will be reviewed and updated. Any legal duties will be assessed for the actual business activities and applicable jurisdiction.

Contact

For questions about the website, use the Contact page. This page reflects the site's current functionality and will be updated as the business develops.
