How we think

Good analysis begins with a better question.

Technology is only useful when it helps people make a decision. We put the problem first, then select the data, methods and tools that fit the context.

  • Evidence-led methods with clear assumptions
  • Fit-for-purpose satellite and spatial datasets
  • Transparent validation and documented limitations
  • Results presented for technical and non-technical audiences
