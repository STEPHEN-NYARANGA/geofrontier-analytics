Our perspective

Useful analysis starts with the right question.

Better evidence can change how we manage land, monitor infrastructure and respond to environmental challenges. Our approach brings together reliable data, sound methods and a practical understanding of how results will be used.

Two specialist starting points

Clear questions. Verifiable evidence.

Our initial focus is analytical support for regional climate decisions and early-stage ground-motion screening. Work is scoped to the available evidence, site and decision rather than promised as a ready-made operational product.

Climate and forecast evidence

What do the coming weeks or season mean for our locations?

Interpret recognised forecasts alongside historical observations, local thresholds and uncertainty. A tailored brief can describe the outlook, its limitations and practical questions for the client to consider.

Explore a climate brief
Radar ground-motion screening

Is there evidence that the ground at our site is moving?

Assess Sentinel-1 InSAR feasibility, identify coherent observations where possible, examine atmospheric and geometric artefacts, and recommend further investigation where needed.

Explore InSAR screening

GPS/GNSS field mapping, satellite analysis, and education remain complementary capabilities. We do not claim licensed land surveying or definitive site-safety certification.
